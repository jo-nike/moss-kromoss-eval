## Failure modes

1. **The key side is right; what the light does downstream is not.** The stated source side landed almost every time (F002, F010, F011, F013, F038). Cast-shadow direction passed only 2 of 9 checks: no long shadows (F006), sideways shadows at "noon" (F005), umbrella shadows pointing different ways (F027). Small effects drop: phone glow (F010), dust motes (F013), caustics (F029, F046).
2. **Named patterns become generic.** The Rembrandt triangle is a broad lit cheek (F001). Butterfly lighting produced flat brown cheek patches (F004, score 2). Split lighting worked (F002). Only 3 portraits.
3. **Lighting gear is drawn into the frame:** a light bar behind the boxer (F003), a reflector dish under the chin (F004).
4. **Camera motion works; subject action doesn't.** Trails, panning and frozen droplets landed (F018, F019, F035, F049). No mid-turn (F011), no mid-shake (F037), the skater rides the lip (F022), the front wheel isn't blurred (F019).
5. **Fine texture becomes high-contrast speckle:** concrete as black dots (F043), velvet as black blotches (F044), leaves as confetti (F042), rocks pitted like snow (F016). Possibly a distillation artefact (needs a non-DMD comparison).
6. **Visible weather works; optical weather fails.** Fog, snow, lightning and aurora are excellent (F015, F038, F012, F040). Heat shimmer and mirage are missing (F039), and the wall cloud is vague (F041).
7. **Sets overfill** (as in set B): four bowls for three (F008), six umbrellas for five (F027).
8. **Unrequested text:** a watermark (F002), garbled signs (F021, F022, F045).

## What correlated with success

- **Length did not matter:** 30 words or fewer 3.83 (n=6), 31-130 3.87 (15), 131-200 4.0 (25), over 200 3.75 (4).
- **Objects beat light behaviour.** Products and food average 4.67 (n=6), with all three requested strings correct ("NOCTURNE", "AUTOMATIC", "18"). F050's tiny "3:07" is illegible.
- **Focus is the most reliable control.** A focus plane named by object worked (F017, third of six jars), as did tilt-shift (F021) and macro (F020).
- **Animals render well but don't pose.** Fur, eyes and pupils are right (F034, F035, F036). Placement and action miss: dog on the wrong side (F006), spider legs not raised (F020), deer barely visible (F015).

## Prompting advice

1. State the light's side; it is followed. Describe where shadows fall in frame and check them, running seeds if they matter.
2. Don't rely on Rembrandt or butterfly by name. Describing shadow shapes directly is untested.
3. Leave out modifiers ("softbox", "reflector"). Describe the light on the subject instead (untested).
4. Use camera techniques freely: DOF, tilt-shift, long exposure, panning, fisheye and freeze-frame all work.
5. Expect action subjects to come out static. Describing visible results (hair fanned out, spray flying sideways) is untested.
6. Inspect fine irregular textures (concrete, crushed velvet, leaf litter) closely.
7. Keep text short and large; tiny display digits failed (one case).
8. Expect no mirage or heat haze (one image).
