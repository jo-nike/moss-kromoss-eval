## Failure modes

1. **Groups of five or more people miscount, usually adding one.** B005 has 8 for 7 (an extra groomsman), B007 5 for 4 (a second grey-bearded guitarist), B039 8 for 6 and B041 17 for 15. B006 is the one undercount (5 children). The extra usually copies a described role.
2. **Sets fill to their default size.** The carton got 11 eggs and 1 empty cup instead of 9 and 3 (B033). The sill got 4 geraniums, not 3 (B050), and the stadium 2 floodlights, not 4 towers (B015).
3. **Named poses become the generic version** (as in set A). The tango dip is upright (B013), the arm-wrestle is a stalemate (B010), the players "at the net" stand at the baseline (B032) and the ducklings walk ahead of their mother (B021).
4. **Body-relative left/right is a coin flip** (9 right, 8 wrong across all such details): B001, B009 (twice), B010, B013, B016, B030, B041.
5. **Mirrors and mechanics break.** The reflected stylist stands elsewhere (B023), and the oars run along the hull (B044).
6. **Unasked extra people appear at the edges or in the background** (B012, B021, B027, B034).

## What worked

- **Attribute binding is excellent.** No clothing swaps in seven-person lineups (B022, B005) or the six-person dinner (B008). The two small leaks are B017's yellow T-shirt and B040's red hat.
- **Order by frame position is reliable** (88%, 0 fails).
- **13 of 14 quoted strings rendered**, including "CAFÉ LUNA" with its accent and "NEXT TRAIN 2 MIN". B042's banner was dropped. Unasked jersey and bib text is gibberish.
- **Interaction hands are clean** (B003, B010, B012, B014, B024), and **crowd faces held up** (B015, B016, B018, B039, B040).

## What correlated with success

- **Describing each member beat giving a bare number.** Fully described groups mostly landed (B004, B008, B022, B029, B036, B042). Undescribed groups of five or more missed (B006, B017, B039, B041). B005 and B007 are exceptions.
- **Length barely mattered.** 30 words or fewer: 4.4 (n=5); over 200 words: 4.0 (n=4).
- **Illustration beat photoreal again:** 4.43 vs 4.05, from only 7 illustrations.

## Prompting advice

1. For five or more people, describe each one in frame order. Expect one extra anyway; run several seeds.
2. Don't rely on partial fills such as "9 of 12 cups"; this rests on one image (B033).
3. Use frame positions, not "her right hand".
4. Describe the visible geometry of a pose instead of naming it (untested).
5. Short quoted text is safe on a large surface. B042's, placed on a tower, was dropped.
6. Try "no other people" against edge extras (untested).
